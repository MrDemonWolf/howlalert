01

Product & Voice

A friendly blue wolf who keeps tabs on your Claude usage. Playful but technical. Reassuring, never panicked.

Voice Rules

  • Plain language first, technical terms after.
  • Never "monitoring" — use "watching" or "keeping tabs".
  • Status uses natural time: "Runs out in 47m" not "47 minutes until limit".
  • Errors stay calm: "Couldn't reach Claude. Trying again in 30s."
  • Exclamation points are reserved for clear wins ("Reset just hit").
  • No emoji. The wolf mark is the only visual personality.

Say · Don’t Say

DORuns out in 47m
NO47 minutes until limit reached
DOKeeping tabs on Claude
NOMonitoring your Claude usage
DOCouldn't reach Claude. Trying again in 30s.
NOError: connection failed (ECONNREFUSED)
DOReset just hit!
NOQuota limit refresh successful 🎉

07

Liquid Glass

Apple’s dynamic meta-material — translucency, lensing, specular highlights. Reserve it for the navigation layer that floats above your content.
Three-Layer Architecture · governing principle
Overlay
Vibrancy & fills on glass — labels, icons, accents inside floating controls. .foregroundStyle(.tint)
Navigation · Liquid Glass
Toolbars, popovers, floating CTAs, tab bars, sheets. One layer only. Glass cannot sample other glass. .glassEffect()
Content
Cards, list rows, body text, charts, imagery. Solid surfaces. Never glass.
Apple HIG

“Liquid Glass is best reserved for the navigation layer that floats above the content of your app.”

  • One glass layer. Header glass or footer glass — not both stacked.
  • Lensing, not blur. Light bends and concentrates; it does not scatter.
  • Concentric corners. Inner glass matches outer container radius. .containerConcentric
  • Cyan tint = primary action. Tint conveys meaning, not decoration. .tint(.cyan)

Six rules

Treat these as non-negotiable. Glass that breaks them feels cheap.

  • Navigation layer only. Never on content cards or list rows.
  • Always over real content — bokeh, photo, or layered art.
  • Adjacent glass merges via a virtual GlassEffectContainer.
  • 6% cyan tint + 1px inner highlight (.tint(.cyan)).
  • Lensing, not blur. Specular top edge, soft bottom shadow.
  • Pressed state shifts highlight ~3px and dims shadow.

Reference CSS for designers + Claude Code: backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%); background: rgba(15,172,237,.06); border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.08); box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.15), 0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,.4);
