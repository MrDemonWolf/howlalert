G.4

Layered icon · iOS 26 / tvOS

Three layers with parallax. Background tilts independently of the wolf when the phone is rotated. Subtle, never busy.
SAFE AREA

Three-layer composition

iOS 26 layered icons need explicit layers so the OS can apply parallax + dynamic lighting. Our composition keeps the wolf as the only animated layer.

  • Background — cyan-to-navy gradient + top highlight. Static.
  • Safe area guide — 12% inset, never visible. Just for export tooling.
  • Foreground — wolf silhouette in white. Catches the parallax — wolf "floats" slightly above the gradient when tilted.
