Watching, not monitoring

The wolf sits at the edge of your workspace and looks after your limits so you don’t have to.

Natural time

“Runs out in 47m” > “47 minutes until limit”.

Calm errors

No panic. Couldn’t reach Claude. Trying again in 30s.

Wins only get exclamations

“Reset just hit!” — and nothing else.

A

Design system

Tokens, components, motion. Every screen consumes these — never hard-code hex.

A1

Brand & voice

Friendly blue wolf — guide, not guard dog.

Voice rules

  • Plain language first, technical terms after.
  • Never monitoring. Use watching or keeping tabs.
  • Status uses natural time: Runs out in 47m.
  • Errors stay calm: Couldn’t reach Claude. Trying again in 30s.
  • No exclamation points except wins (Reset just hit!).

A5

Liquid Glass

Navigation layer only. Lensing, not blur. 6% cyan tint, top inner highlight.

Open HowlAlert

5-Hour Window

Rules

  • Navigation layer only — popovers, tabs, toolbars, floating CTAs.
  • Glass must sit over real content. Never on solid navy without bokeh underneath.
  • Two glass elements near each other? Merge highlights along the shared edge.
  • Cyan tint via .tint(.cyan) — 6% overlay, 1px inner highlight.
  • Lensing, not blur. Specular top edge, soft bottom shadow.
  • Pressed state shifts highlight 3px down.

B

macOS — menu bar app

SwiftUI MenuBarExtra(.window). Liquid Glass dropdown, 320pt wide. Multi-provider — Claude + Gemini stacked on one Overview, plus a tab per provider.

B1

Menu bar icon · 22 × 22 template

Wolf head silhouette as the template image. Monochrome at rest, state-tinted on alert.

Idle

Outline wolf · template image, system tints to current menu bar fg.

Warning · 70–89% used

Filled amber wolf. Solid, no pulse — calm signal.

Critical · 90%+ used

Filled red wolf with a 2s pulse ring. The only animated state.

Template images render correctly against any wallpaper — color is supplied via the system at idle, only forced at warning/critical.

SwiftUI hookup

All five states share one ActivityAttributes. Compact-active bar binds to the same UsageProvider as the iOS hero — a single source of truth.

.minimal · ContentView only — wolf glyph state-tinted
.compact · wolf leading + depleting bar + % trailing
.expanded · DynamicIslandExpandedRegion(.leading/.trailing/.center/.bottom)
.lockScreen · ActivityViewContext — glass card, tap to deeplink://dashboard

Bar in compact-active depletes per spec: cyan when fresh (above 30% left), amber under 30% left (70% used), red under 10% left (90% used). State color crosses thresholds smoothly via linear 250ms.
