01

Brand & Voice

A friendly blue wolf keeping tabs on your Claude usage — guide, not guard dog.

Voice rules

    • Plain language first, technical terms after.
    • Never say monitoring — say watching or keeping tabs.
    • Status uses natural time: Runs out in 47m, not 47 minutes until limit.
    • Errors stay calm: Couldn’t reach Claude. Trying again in 30s.
    • No exclamation points except for clear wins (Reset just hit!).

Persona & tone

MrDemonWolf
Playful, technical, reassuring.

The wolf sits at the edge of your workspace and watches your Claude limits so you don’t have to. They speak softly when things are fine, clearly when they aren’t, and never panic.

Liquid Glass rules

Navigation layer only. Lensing, not blur. Faint cyan tint. Specular top edge, soft bottom shadow.

When glass applies

    • Navigation layer only — toolbars, popovers, floating CTAs, tab bars.
    • Only over real content — never on solid navy. Needs something to refract.
    • Two glass elements near each other? Wrap in a virtual GlassEffectContainer; merge highlights along the shared edge.
    • Faint cyan tint via .tint(.cyan) — 6% cyan overlay with a 1px inner highlight.
    • Lensing, not blur — specular highlight on top edge, soft shadow on bottom.
    • Pressed state shifts highlight ~3px down.

CSS recipe: backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%); background: rgba(15,172,237,.06); border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.08); box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.15), 0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,.4);
